TaxRAG: Indian Income Tax Q&A assistant

  • RAG over the full Income-Tax Act, 2025: 477 sections, 1,237 passages, answers cited by section, plus on-the-fly indexing of uploaded PDFs.
  • Three fine-tuned sentence-transformer retrievers (tax, finance, Hinglish): Recall@5 0.96, MRR@5 0.85 on a 100-query hard set.
  • LLM answers served through automatic failover across Groq, NVIDIA and Kilo endpoints; an abstention gate refuses 11 of 12 out-of-scope questions.

Customer churn prediction and retention budget optimization

  • 1.76M e-commerce purchase records aggregated to 5,013 customers × 24 features, with temporal train/test cutoffs.
  • Voting ensemble: ROC-AUC 0.898, top-decile precision 0.487, AUC 0.87–0.89 across 30–180-day churn windows; SHAP drivers.
  • Retention budget allocated as a 0/1-knapsack MILP in HiGHS, plus survival analysis of time-to-return and A/B test power design.

QuantRisk: portfolio risk analytics dashboard

  • Live NSE, US and global portfolios via yfinance, with benchmark alpha, tracking error and information ratio.
  • 95% / 99% VaR and Expected Shortfall by historical, parametric and Monte Carlo methods; drawdown and per-asset risk contribution.
  • Fama-French 5-factor regression and an efficient frontier of max-Sharpe, minimum-volatility and risk-parity portfolios.

Credit default prediction: AmEx, 11-model benchmark

  • 458,913 customers with up to 13 monthly statements × 188 features; leakage-free customer-level split.
  • 11 models including 1D-CNN, LSTM, GRU, CNN-LSTM, Transformer and XGBoost: ROC-AUC 0.960, PR-AUC 0.894, confirmed with 1,000 paired bootstrap resamples.
  • GRU-embedding + XGBoost hybrid, and K-Means risk tiers with default rates from 2.6% to 93.2%.

TxCat: transaction categorization and anomaly detection

  • 200,000 real bank transaction descriptions sorted into 10 spending categories.
  • TF-IDF with Logistic Regression and XGBoost: 97.5% accuracy, unchanged on a split with zero merchant overlap (53,160 merchants).

MRSS: mortgage risk segmentation and survival analysis

  • 62,359 FHFA mortgages (2013–2024) on 17 origination features, segmented with PCA and K-Means and cross-checked by Ward clustering.
  • Recovers a prime tier (FICO 773, LTV 56%) and a higher-risk tier (FICO 734, LTV 84%).
  • Kaplan-Meier, log-rank and Cox PH on 90-day delinquency: C-index 0.858, and 0.902 on 2021–2024 out-of-time loans.
